A NOTE FROM THE WRITER

8 Months In

Sayang, I keep coming back to how this started. A random night at KLIA, a McDonald's bag between us, me pointing out planes you didn't ask about. I didn't know that was the pilot episode of anything. I just knew I didn't want the night to end.

Eight months later and it's still the same feeling, just with more evidence now. A cherry blossom house we built together, axolotls with our names on them, a Snivy plush sitting next to your letter on my desk. Every "episode" since has just been us choosing to keep going, even from far away, even on the hard nights on call.

We're still climbing toward whatever comes after this. I don't mind taking it slow with you. Happy 8 months, Sayang. Renewed indefinitely.

Yours, always. Khairul
