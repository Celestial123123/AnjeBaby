A NOTE FROM THE WRITER

8 Months In

Sayang, I keep coming back to how this started. A random night at KLIA, a McDonald's bag between us, you pointing out planes that was hard to see because it was night. I didn't know that was the pilot episode of anything. I just knew I didn't want the night to end.

Eight months later and it's still the same feeling, just with more evidence now. A cherry blossom house we built together, axolotls with our names on them, a Snivy plushie attached to your bag alongside the toothless keychain. Every "episode" since has just been us choosing to keep going, even from far away, even on the hard nights on call.

We're still climbing toward whatever comes after this. I don't mind taking it slow with you. Happy 8 months, Sayang. I love you so so much, Nur Liyana Jasmine.

Yours, always. Khairul
