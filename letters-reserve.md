# Thirty letters

Written in advance as a reserve. Use them as they are, change them, or ignore
them and write your own on the day.

---

## 01

Sayangg, this is the first one.

There's going to be one of these every single day. I already wrote them all out.

You don't have to read them on time. You don't have to reply to any of them. If you open one a week late that's fine too.

They're just going to be here every day, whether you're okay or not okay.

I love you so much.

## 02

Morning sayangg.

This one doesn't need anything from you. No reply, no smile, nothing at all.

If today is a bad day you can close this right now and it still counts. I mean that.

You don't have to earn these.

## 03

I still think about the fact that we met in a game.

If I was stubborn and didn't come back to the game, none of this would have existed.

No you, no us, nothing. I think about that more than I should.

I got lucky, sayangg. That's the whole thought.

## 04

My internship starts today sayangg.

7am to 8pm, every day. I'm telling you properly now so that when I'm slow to reply you know exactly what it is.

It's not you. It's never going to be you.

I'll still be here every night. I'll just be tired and probably typing like an idiot.

Don't let me get away with disappearing, okay?

## 05

Your voice is the best part of my whole day sayangg.

It doesn't even matter what you're saying. You could be talking about nothing, complaining about class, telling me the same story twice. I don't care.

If I had a bad day, the moment you start talking it's gone.

Even when you're annoyed at me. Especially then actually, because at least I'm getting the real you.

I just like hearing you sayangg. That's the whole letter.

## 06

If today is one of the bad ones:

You don't have to explain it to me. You don't have to tell me why. You don't have to be okay by tonight, or tomorrow either.

You don't have to make it make sense, because sometimes it doesn't.

I'm not going anywhere sayangg. I'll just sit here and wait it out with you for as long as it takes.

## 07

You're studying something properly hard sayangg.

Most people wouldn't even attempt it. And you still show up for it on the days you feel like absolute nothing.

You drag yourself there and you do it anyway.

I don't think you ever give yourself a single bit of credit for that. So I'm giving it to you instead.

## 08

You're funny sayangg. Genuinely funny.

Not trying to be funny, not doing a bit. Just the random things you come out with.

I think about them hours later and laugh by myself like a weirdo.

You do that to me almost every day and you don't even notice.

## 09

I hope today was boring sayangg.

Not bad, not big, nothing happening. Just a completely normal day where nothing hurt and nothing went wrong.

I know that sounds like a low thing to wish for someone.

But I'd take a hundred boring days for you over one hard one.

## 10

A question, no reason behind it:

If tomorrow was completely free, no class, nobody needing anything from you at all, what would you actually do with it?

Not what you think you should do. Not the productive answer.

What you actually want sayangg. I want to know.

## 11

You told me once that you're a house, and that if I keep hitting it, it's going to break.

You're not going to break sayangg.

And even if something cracks, I'm not the type to walk out and go find a different house.

I'd just fix it. I'd keep fixing it.

That's what I signed up for.

## 12

KLIA.

I had waited so long for that day that part of me genuinely thought it wasn't going to happen. Like something would go wrong at the last second.

Then you were just there, right in front of me, and I didn't know what to do with myself.

I still think about it sayangg. I probably always will.

## 13

Did you eat today sayangg?

I'm not asking to nag you, I promise. I'm asking because I actually want to know.

If you didn't, please go eat something. It doesn't have to be a proper meal.

Chocolate pun boleh. Anything.

## 14

Some days nothing is wrong and you still feel bad. That just happens.

It doesn't mean something is broken sayangg, and it doesn't mean you ruined anything.

You don't have to find a reason for it. You don't have to explain it to me or to yourself.

Some days are just like that. It passes.

## 15

I don't need anything from you today sayangg. No reply, no update, nothing.

Not because I don't care. The opposite actually.

I've thought about you about ten times before lunch and I lost count after that.

I just don't want you feeling like you owe me a message on top of everything else you're carrying.

## 16

I keep wanting to tell you small things that aren't worth telling anyone else.

What I ate. Something stupid someone said in class. That I was thinking about you on the way home for no reason.

Nobody else would care about any of it sayangg.

You're the only person I want to tell.

## 17

You don't owe me a good mood sayangg.

You have never had to pretend to be okay for me, not once, and you never will.

If it has ever felt like you did, that was me doing something wrong, not you.

You can be in the worst mood of your life and I still want to be in the call.

## 18

Something I noticed about you:

You always check on other people first. Even when you're the one who's completely wrecked, you still ask if they're okay.

You did it that night too. You were the one falling apart and you still asked me.

I don't think you even know you do it sayangg. But I see it every time.

## 19

You're going to be a good vet sayangg.

Not because you're clever, even though you are.

Because you actually care what happens to them. You think about them after you go home.

You can't teach that part. People either have it or they don't, and you have it.

## 20

Her by JVKE.

I can't listen to it without thinking about you. The whole song is just you sayangg.

Put it on when you have a minute.

## 21

About the bad nights.

I don't hold any of it against you. Not one word of it. I never did and I never will.

We were both on the floor that night. People say things from down there that they don't mean.

It stays there sayangg. It doesn't follow us.

## 22

Nur Liyana Jasmine.

Sometimes I type out your full name just to look at it.

I don't know why. It just makes it feel more real that you're mine.

That's all this one is.

## 23

I think about normal things with you sayangg. Not big plans.

Just being in the same room, doing nothing, not having to say goodnight through a phone.

One day it won't be a visit. It'll just be a normal day.

That's the part I'm waiting for.

## 24

If you're tired today, be tired sayangg.

You don't have to climb out of it by tonight. You don't have to be better tomorrow either.

Tomorrow's letter is going to be here anyway. So am I.

Rest properly, okay?

## 25

You told me about this flower when we first started talking. Queen of the night.

I never forgot it. I don't think you even remember telling me.

So that's what's on this page sayangg, and it opens a little bit more every day I write to you.

It's yours. It was always going to be yours.

## 26

You're allowed to be angry with me sayangg.

If I do something that deserves it, I would so much rather you tell me than sit there holding it in.

I can handle being told I'm wrong. I've been told worse.

What I can't handle is you going quiet and me not knowing what I did.

## 27

One promise I can actually keep, so this is the only one I'm making:

When you tell me something hurt you, I'm going to listen to all of it before I say a single thing about myself.

No getting defensive. No making it about me.

That's it. No big speech sayangg. Just that one.

## 28

Thank you for the things nobody sees sayangg.

The gift you bought me before you could really afford it. Staying up with me when you had a 6am the next day.

Sending me that video instead of keeping it to yourself, when saying nothing would have been so much easier.

I noticed all of it. Every single one.

## 29

That's the flower fully open sayangg.

One whole month. I didn't miss a single day, not even once.

I told you it wouldn't just be words.

This is me showing you, slowly, in the most boring way possible, every day, whether you were okay or not.

## 30

And it keeps going after this.

The flower is full but I'm not done sayangg. There will be more of these. They just stop being a countdown.

I love you. Same as day one.

Except now there's thirty days of proof sitting right there.
